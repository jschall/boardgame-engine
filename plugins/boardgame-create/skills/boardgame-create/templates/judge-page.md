---
You are reviewing a single-page HTML proposal for a laser-cut board game. Open {page path} in a browser if you can (playwright), otherwise use the screenshots at {paths}. Report concrete defects only.

Required, in this order: (0) a compact hero: title, one line, pills, and nothing nobody will read (no lede paragraph, no links); (1) the 3D stage: one full-viewport canvas that sticks while the opening plays as the reader scrolls (two closed boxes, one dissolves, the table fades in, the box lies down, the lid flies to its place, every packed piece flies to the table; a Scroll hint with Skip; it must run backwards and the page must scroll past it), then tabs The table / Parts (no box tab), Reset view, and the chips, a fixed-height scrolling log that is the only place action text appears, and Play / Pause / New game / speed overlaid on the canvas; (2) the rulebook: the real print rulebook as a page-turning book, with the PDF links here and nowhere else; (3) the SVG files: one card per sheet with a preview image and a download link, the colour legend (red cut, blue score, magenta corner marks, black engrave, kerf in the file with machine compensation off), the stock inputs, the production and test sheet groups. Check and list every failure: instruction text (rules, FAQ, aids, card text) that is not plain complete sentences: telegraphic fragments, slogan fragments or phrase stacks, header-colon shorthand, hype adjectives, "not just X, it's Y", rhetorical questions, dash asides, triplets for rhythm, words like delve, journey, elevate, seamless, vibrant, closing summaries that restate; any specialist word a family would not know in text a player reads ("skep" for beehive), including piece names; a section missing or out of order; text that contradicts the rules or the parts (counts, sizes, names); figures that are placeholders or not from the cut files; images too large ("ridiculously huge") or too small to read; the 3D stage not filling the viewport or trapping the scrollbar (the reader must reach the rulebook and the files by scrolling, with the wheel over the canvas and by touch); the opening not reversible, or pieces passing through one another or through the box while it plays, or a piece landing anywhere but its table place; the overlaid panel hiding the subject (home views must sit in the clear part of the canvas); any JavaScript error in the console; the log resizing or action text appearing anywhere else; the parts tab missing any part; any view that shows a duplicated part in two assemblies; the closed box (in the opening and in the parts list Views) not showing its outside engraving, the lid lifting, and the underside when orbited from below (the table must disappear from below); the same rulebook link in two places; captions or copy on the page that nobody would read; textures that visibly repeat; lighting that leaves the top faces dark; a texture that flashes to another on load.

Also read `reference/standards.md` sections G and E4, and report G1–G8 as MET or NOT MET with the screenshot or element that decides each; G8 is judged by scrolling the real page.
